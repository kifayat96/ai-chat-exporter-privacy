# Privacy Policy: AI Chat Exporter

Last updated: October 16, 2026

AI Chat Exporter is a Chrome extension that exports ChatGPT and Claude conversations to PDF, Word and Markdown.

## What data the extension reads
When you click the extension on a ChatGPT or Claude page, it reads the conversation shown on that page so it can create your export file.

## Where your data goes
Nowhere. Your conversations are processed only inside your own browser. They are never sent to us or to any server, and we never see them.

## What is stored on your device
- How many free exports you used today (to apply the daily free limit)
- Your premium license key, if you enter one

This is saved locally in your browser using Chrome storage and is never shared.

## License check
If you enter a premium license key, the extension sends only that key and our product ID to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase is valid. No chat content is ever sent.

## Data sales and tracking
We do not sell, share or transfer user data. We do not use analytics, ads or trackers.
